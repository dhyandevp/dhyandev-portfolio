# Asset & Sticker Provenance

This document records the source URL, license, and terms for all brand marks, icons, and original artwork used as stickers in the **Off the clock** photo collage.

## Brand & Tool Stickers

All brand logos are reproduced faithfully without distortion, redrawing, or modification beyond the monochrome/official color allowances in their respective brand guidelines. Each tile displays a tooltip stating **"Tools I use: [Tool Name]"** with no endorsement or partnership implied.

- **Source**: Anthropic Brand Kit & Simple Icons (`anthropic.svg`)

- **Terms / Guidelines**: GitHub Logos and Usage Guidelines. Reproduction in solid white on dark tile background `#111110` with required clear space.
